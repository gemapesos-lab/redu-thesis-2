Just one more video. Suddenly, it’s two-oh-seven AM.

That’s doomscrolling. Screen-time limits count minutes, not what you watch.

Meet REDU, an Android app that helps you pause.

Built for adult Filipino Android users.

Three signals: session length, time per video, and negative captions. It checks Filipino and English text for negative language.

Fuzzy logic combines them into a doomscrolling risk score.

When needed: a reminder, a pause, or a breathing break. You stay in control.

Processed on-device. Raw content isn’t saved.

In a two-week pilot with fifty Filipino adults, prompted users scrolled less and saw less negative content.

REDU. Notice the scroll. Choose the pause.
